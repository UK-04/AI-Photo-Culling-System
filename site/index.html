What it does

  • Catches the misses

    Out-of-focus frames, camera shake, closed eyes and badly exposed shots, each with the reason.

  • Stacks bursts

    Similar frames fold into one stack with the sharpest, eyes-open frame on top.

  • Sorts by topic

    Group photos, couples, details, venue and more, for weddings, events, portraits and travel.

  • Learns your taste

    Every pick and reject teaches it, so the next shoot arrives closer to how you cull.

  • Built for the keyboard

    P pick, X reject, 1–5 stars, click to zoom to 100%.

  • Hands off to Lightroom

    Exports colour labels and stars as sidecars, or copies your picks to a folder.

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded Culler.dmg and drag Culler into Applications.
  2. Open Culler from Applications. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Culler, then confirm.

You only do this once. Culler is not yet registered with Apple, which is why macOS asks. After the first launch it opens normally and keeps itself up to date.

Culler is open source. Source code, and every version is on GitHub.
